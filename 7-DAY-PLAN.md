# 7-Day Plan

## Day 1
- Turn on GitHub Pages (Settings > Pages > main, root).
- Fill in your name, email, phone on the landing page.
- Set up a free payment link (Stripe Payment Link, PayPal, or Cash App) for $100 (the deposit).
- Build your lead list: 30 businesses from Google Maps with no website.

## Day 2 to 3
- Send 25 messages a day (see OUTREACH.md).
- Make a free preview for anyone who says yes, same day.

## Day 4
- Follow up once with everyone who hasn't replied.
- Message 10 to 20 new leads.

## Day 5 to 7: review checklist
Answer these honestly on day 5:
- [ ] How many messages did I send? (Target: 50+)
- [ ] How many replied? (Under 3 replies: change the first line of the message, or try a different trade.)
- [ ] How many previews did I make? (Replies but no previews: lead with the free preview offer.)
- [ ] How many paid? (Previews but no sales: lower to $149, or offer "pay nothing until you love it".)
- [ ] Which channel got the most replies? Do more of that one on days 6 and 7.
- [ ] Ask every paying customer for a short review and one referral.

By day 7, aim for 2 to 3 paying customers ($400 to $600) and a few care plan signups ($25/month each).
