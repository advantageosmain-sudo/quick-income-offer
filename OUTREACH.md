# Outreach: Finding and Messaging Your First Customers

Goal: 50 messages in the first 3 days. Expect 2 to 5 replies and 1 to 2 sales. You send every message yourself.

## Where to find leads (all free)

1. **Google Maps.** Search "[trade] near [your town]" (barber, cleaning, food truck, landscaping, detailing). Any listing with no "Website" button is a lead. Note the name, phone, and whether they have reviews.
2. **Facebook.** Local "buy/sell", "small business", and town community groups. Look for businesses posting from a Facebook page with no website link.
3. **Instagram.** Search hashtags like #[yourtown]barber, #[yourtown]eats, #[yourtown]cleaning. A link-in-bio pointing only to a booking app or nothing is a lead.
4. **Nextdoor** business pages and recommendations threads.
5. **People you already know.** Friends, family, your own barber, nail tech, or mechanic. Warm leads close fastest.

Keep a simple list (Google Sheets or notes): business, contact, where found, date messaged, reply, follow-up date.

## Message 1: DM or text (short)

> Hi [Name], I came across [Business] on [Google Maps / Instagram] and love your [specific thing: reviews, photos, menu]. I noticed you don't have a website yet. I build simple, mobile-friendly sites for local businesses, live in 48 hours for $199 flat with no monthly fees. Here's an example: [demo link]. Want me to make a free preview for [Business] so you can see it first?

## Message 2: email (a bit longer)

> **Subject:** A website for [Business]?
>
> Hi [Name],
>
> I found [Business] on [where] and your [reviews/photos] are great. When I searched for you, though, there was no website, which means people searching Google might be calling someone else.
>
> I build one-page websites for local businesses: your services, hours, map, photos, reviews, and tap-to-call buttons. It's live in 48 hours, costs $199 one time, and hosting is free.
>
> Here's an example: [demo link]
>
> If you'd like, I'll put together a free preview for [Business]. No obligation.
>
> [Your name]
> [Phone]

## Follow-up (2 to 3 days later, once)

> Hi [Name], just checking back on this. Happy to send a free preview of a site for [Business] whenever you're ready, takes me about an hour. No pressure either way!

## The "free preview" move

Offering a free preview gets many more yeses. When someone says yes:
1. Ask Claude: "Make a one-page site for [Business], a [trade] in [town], using demo/index.html as the template. Here's what I know: [hours, phone, services, reviews]."
2. Send them the preview link.
3. "Want it live? It's $199, half now and half once you're happy." Send your payment link.

## Answers to common replies

- **"I already have Facebook."** "Facebook is great, and the website links to it. A site shows up on Google searches, where people look first."
- **"Too expensive."** "Most web designers charge $500 to $2,000 plus monthly fees. This is $199 once. One new customer usually pays for it."
- **"Let me think about it."** "Of course. Want me to make the free preview so you have something to look at?"
- **"How do I know it's legit?"** "You only pay half up front, and only after you've seen the preview."
