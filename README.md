# Quick Income Offer: One-Page Websites for Local Businesses

A done-for-you service sold directly to local business owners. AI does most of the building, you sell and deliver. No paid tools, no inventory, no ad spend.

## Why this offer (and not the others)

| Option | Price | Why it ranked where it did |

| **1. One-page website, live in 48 hours** (recommended) | $199 one-time, +$25/mo optional care | Easy to show (send the demo), easy to judge (they can see they have no site or a bad one), fast to deliver with AI, and free to host on GitHub Pages. |

The website offer wins on speed to the first payment: a business owner can see the demo, say yes, and pay the same day.

## The offer

**"Your business online in 48 hours, $199, no monthly fees."**

What the customer gets:
- A clean, mobile-friendly one-page site: what you do, hours, location map, phone/text/email buttons, photos, reviews.
- Hosted free (GitHub Pages). They can add their own domain later.
- One round of edits included.
- Optional: $25/month care plan for updates (new hours, specials, photos).

Payment: 50% up front ($100) to start, 50% on launch. Or $199 up front with a "free if you don't love it" guarantee if you want faster closes.

How to collect money with no overhead: a free Stripe Payment Link, PayPal invoice, Venmo Business, or Cash App. All are free to set up and only take a small fee per payment.

## Who to sell to

Local businesses that have **no website**, or only a Facebook page, or a broken/outdated site. Best fits:
- Food trucks, home bakers, caterers
- Barbers, nail techs, lash and brow artists
- Cleaners, landscapers, handymen, pressure washers
- Mobile mechanics, detailers, movers
- Tutors, personal trainers, photographers

Quick test: search Google Maps for "[trade] near [your town]". Any listing without a "Website" button is a lead.

## Delivery in under 2 hours per client

1. Get from the client: business name, what they do, hours, area/address, phone, 3 to 6 photos, 2 to 3 reviews (copy from Google/Facebook).
2. Copy `demo/index.html` into a new folder and swap in their details. Ask Claude: *"Rewrite this demo site for [business], here are their details: ..."*
3. Put it on GitHub Pages (free) and send them the link.
4. Make their one round of edits, collect the second half.

## What's in this repo

- `index.html`: your sales landing page (what you send people to).
- `demo/index.html`: a sample site for a fictional bakery, to show prospects what they'll get.

Before going live, search `index.html` for `REPLACE` and put in your own name and contact details.
